Good to great in 90 seconds.

If you train hard, eat right and still aren't seeing it in the mirror, it's not effort. It's leverage. Here are the seven levers:

1. Six sets per muscle in a session is the cap. Anything past that adds fatigue without definition.

2. Train each muscle two to three times a week. Once a week and you spend more time losing definition than creating it.

3. Give working sets at least two minutes in between. Shorter than that caps how heavy you can actually lift.

4. Soreness and the pump are false signals. Bar speed slowing on the last rep is the right one.

5. Film your sets. You're probably further from failure than you think.

6. Hunger in week six is harder than week one — that's ghrelin rising. Front-load calories and protein into breakfast and lunch. That buys the longest runway before it hits.

7. A short deficit beats a long slow one. About 1% of body weight each week for five to six weeks, then straight back to maintenance to reset. That's how you dodge the metabolism slowdown.
