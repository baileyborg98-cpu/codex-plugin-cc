Nobody wants to hear this one.

You have spent months being disciplined. Weighing your food. Saying no to things you wanted. And now the advice is to eat more?

I know exactly how that lands. It feels like undoing every bit of work you have put in.

So here is the part the slides do not have room for.

When you bring your calories up, the scale is going to move. Probably two or three kilos in the first week or so.

That is not fat. You cannot gain three kilos of fat in a week, not on the calories we are talking about. It is food volume, water, and glycogen refilling muscle that has been running on empty for months.

But almost everyone panics at that number and dives straight back down to 1,400. And that is the exact moment this fails. Not the eating more. The bailing out.

You have to sit through it. Give it a few weeks. The scale settles. Your energy comes back. Your training gets sharper. Your steps go up without you even thinking about it. And your maintenance starts climbing with all of it.

Then, and only then, do you have a deficit worth running.

The hardest part of this was never the food. It is holding your nerve for a few weeks while the scale does something you do not like.

If you have been sat at 1,400 for longer than you would admit to anyone, this is the way out.
