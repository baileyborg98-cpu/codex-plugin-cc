# Straight Face

Deadpan batch. Carries on from the cookie dough one. Same engine every time: say the thing with a completely flat face while the visual does the arguing for you. One idea per script, one breath, hard land.

---

## 1. Supplements

**On screen:** what supplements do you take

Every week someone asks what I'm taking, what the stack is, what the one thing was that changed everything, and it's creatine, five grams, same tub since March, that's the whole fucking cabinet, and I know you wanted a longer list because a list feels like you're already doing something, but you're out here shopping for sprinkles and you haven't made the cake.

**Land:** Five grams, baby. The rest is you.

**Delivery:** Hold the tub up, don't present it, just have it in your hand like you were mid scoop when the camera turned on. Kitchen. Flat all the way through, no lift on the land.

---

## 2. Tried everything

**On screen:** "i've tried everything"

People tell me they've tried absolutely everything and then we sit down and actually go through it and it's keto for nine days, a shake thing for a fortnight, an app opened twice, four gyms joined across four separate Januaries, and every single one of them died before it ever got boring enough to work.

**Land:** You've got a graveyard of week ones, my friend.

**Delivery:** Sat down, phone propped, counting them off on your fingers slowly like you're genuinely tallying. Land goes quieter, not louder.

---

## 3. The routine

**On screen:** the optimal morning routine

So the routine is wake at five, journal, meditate, cold plunge, twenty minutes of mobility, sunlight straight in the eyes, first meal at nine, and I'm reading this at twenty past four in the morning with a teething seven month old asleep on my shoulder who has decided that sleeping is something other families do, so we'll be running a modified version today where I remain upright.

**Land:** The routine is whatever survives the night.

**Delivery:** Film it when it actually happens. Dark room, one lamp, whisper the whole thing. If Delilah stirs, keep rolling, that take is the take.

---

## 4. The scale

**On screen:** up 1.5kg since friday

Stepped on the scale this morning a kilo and a half up from Friday, which means either I found three thousand extra calories a day across a weekend where I had one curry and slept badly, or, and stay with me on this one, I am a large bag of water that had salt in it and went to bed late.

**Land:** You weighed the curry, baby.

**Delivery:** Stood on the scale for the whole thing, looking down at it, not at the camera. Look up only for the land.

---

## 5. Clean eating

**On screen:** cleaning my food

Got told my diet isn't clean enough so I'm washing this donut, giving it a proper rinse, really getting in the ridges, because somewhere along the way food picked up a moral status and this one has apparently been out doing crime.

**Land:** It's a donut. Eat it or don't.

**Delivery:** Actually rinse it under the tap. Full commitment, zero smirk. The bit only works if you never break. Bin shot optional.

---

## 6. Discipline

**On screen:** discipline

Everyone wants discipline to be this monk thing where you rise before the sun and conquer the day, and I'm stood at the sink at eleven at night eating cereal out the fucking box because I forgot to have a proper dinner, and I still trained, still worked, still got through the list.

**Land:** Nobody's a monk. Do it anyway.

**Delivery:** Kitchen, late, overhead light only. Talk with your mouth half full. Do not clean up for this one.

---

## 7. What I eat in a day

**On screen:** what i eat in a day, honest version

Honest version, no ring light, no bowl of purple stuff. Coffee, then more coffee, eggs on toast eaten standing up, whatever Delilah didn't finish off her tray, chicken and rice at some point because it's quick and I stopped needing lunch to be a moment, then a proper dinner with my wife and something sweet after because I'm a human being.

**Land:** That's the diet. That's the whole physique.

**Delivery:** Cut between each one as you say it, real footage, phone in one hand. Nothing plated nicely. The plainer it looks the better it works.

---

## 8. Lighting

**On screen:** same body, 8 seconds apart

Took a progress photo in the bathroom this morning and looked like I'd never lifted anything heavier than my phone, walked four steps into the kitchen where the sun comes through the window and suddenly I'm carved out of marble, same body, same morning, eight seconds apart.

**Land:** The lightbulb did that. Not you.

**Delivery:** Show both photos. This one lives or dies on the cut, so shoot the two stills first and build the talking round them.

---

## Notes

Every one of these is a visual with a voiceover, not a talking head with a prop. If you filmed number five as a piece to camera holding a donut it dies. Rinsing it under a tap while never once acknowledging the joke is the whole thing.

Numbers 3 and 7 are the two that carry Delilah. Don't post them in the same week, the dad angle gets diluted if it turns up too often.

Number 8 needs the least talking of the batch. If it feels long when you read it out, cut the middle clause and let the two photos do it.
