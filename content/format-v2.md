# The two formats, v2

Two engines, and they are deliberately nothing like each other. The education ones send someone away with a job. The pub ones send someone away having had a good chat.

---

## EDUCATION v2: the fix is the point

**What was wrong with v1.** Every script diagnosed the problem beautifully and then landed on a philosophy line. "You can't rush a body that doesn't trust you." Great line. Now what? The viewer nods, agrees, and changes nothing, because nothing was ever asked of them.

**The new shape.**

```
Hook, a specific situation with a number, by second three
Mechanism, why it's happening, one metaphor maximum
THE TURN: "so here's what you actually do"
The fix, in real numbers, for long enough to matter
Land that points at the fix, never away from it
```

The fix is the longest section now, not a tag at the end. It gets 10 to 15 seconds and it names actual numbers: grams, sets, reps, days, weeks. The masterclass notes give us all of them, so nothing has to be vague.

**Two rules for the fix.**

It has to be doable tonight without buying anything or asking anyone. And it has to be small enough to say in three beats. If the fix needs a consultation, it's not a fix, it's an advert.

**The land changed job.** It used to be the punchline. Now it's the instruction said one more time, shorter. "Bodyweight in kilos times two. Go and work yours out." Somebody can close the app on that and do it.

---

## PUB YAP: life, motivation, hobbies, travel

**What was wrong with v1.** They were written. Composed. Every sentence doing a job. They read like someone reading you something rather than someone telling you something.

**The new shape.** It isn't a shape, that's the point. It's a bloke talking.

- Start mid answer, like someone just asked you. "People ask me about the move all the time." "Someone called me delusional last year." "Right, cheerful one this."
- Own the digression rather than editing it out
- Repeat yourself the way people actually do. "Two days. Two days."
- "Honestly", "mate", "genuinely", "right", "is there", "was it", "by the way"
- Let sentences be a bit messy. "And I was a bit annoyed at first, right, then I sat with it and thought, no, fair enough."
- Ask the room a question you then answer yourself
- No fix, no lesson, no call to action. It ends where a story ends.

**The one thing that stays disciplined** is the beat clock. Pub speech is fast, so two to three second beats are natural here, not a constraint.

**The land is still hard.** A pub yap that trails off is just rambling. You get to the thing and you stop on it.

---

## Why keep them so far apart

The education content earns the authority and sells the coaching. The pub content earns the trust and travels cold. Your own numbers showed it: the Corfu one hit 98% non followers, the abs one hit 93.6% and got the comments.

If the pub ones start teaching, they stop being a conversation. If the education ones start philosophising, they stop being useful. The gap between them is the whole strategy.
