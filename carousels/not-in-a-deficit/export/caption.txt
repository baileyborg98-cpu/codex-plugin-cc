"I'm eating in a deficit and I'm not losing weight."

You are not. I promise you.

And I want to be careful here, because this is where most people either get told they are lazy, or get told their metabolism is broken and neither one is true or useful.

Here is the honest version.

A deficit is not a feeling. It is not eating clean. It is not being good all week. It is not being hungry. You can be hungry, eat clean, train hard and still not be in one. Those things feel like a deficit. They are not evidence of one.

So before you decide your body is the exception, rule out the boring stuff.

The number came from a calculator that has never met you. The plan came from someone with a different body, a different job and a different amount of daily movement. Or you have been estimating portions by eye, and eyes are famously terrible at it. Most people underestimate what they eat by a long way, and they are not lying, they genuinely cannot see it.

And if you have PCOS, a thyroid condition or insulin resistance, that is real. It lowers what you burn. It makes this harder and slower and more annoying than it is for the person next to you, and it is completely fair to be frustrated about that.

But it lowers your number. It does not repeal the mechanism.

Which is actually the good news. If the rule still applies to you, it means there is still a lever. It just needs to be set to your number rather than somebody else's.

Find your number. Then hold the gap.
