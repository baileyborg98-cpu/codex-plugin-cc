# Aldi Shopping List — one week, two people

Covers every meal in [the meal plan](meal-plan.md), plus the snack and post-workout sections. Aldi own-brand names given where they're stable — anything not in stock, the equivalent from the same aisle does the job.

## Meat & fish
- [ ] Chicken breast fillets — **~2.8kg** (2 large family packs / 3 × 1kg) — katsu, salt & pepper chicken, fried chicken burgers, pizza topping, batch-cooked lunches
- [ ] Chicken thigh fillets — **1 pack (~600g)** — doner kebab wraps
- [ ] 5% lean beef mince — **500g** — smash burgers
- [ ] Beef frying steak / stir-fry strips — **~450g** (or a second 500g pack of 5% mince) — crispy chilli beef
- [ ] Bacon medallions — **1 pack** — Sunday bagels
- [ ] Tuna in spring water — **2 tins** — bagel melts

## Bakery (Village Bakery)
- [ ] Brioche burger buns — **1 pack of 4** (Tue + Sun burger nights)
- [ ] Large tortilla wraps — **2 packs of 6** — lunches + doner night
- [ ] Bagels — **2 packs of 5** — lunches, Sunday breakfast, post-workout
- [ ] Sourdough loaf — **1** — post-workout toast & honey
- [ ] Flatbreads / thin pizza bases — **1–2 packs** — Friday pizza night

## Dairy & chilled
- [ ] Semi-skimmed milk — **2 × 4 pints** (Cowbelle)
- [ ] Skimmed milk — **1 × 2 pints** — post-workout cereal bowls
- [ ] Fat-free Greek-style yogurt — **2 × 500g** (Brooklea) — overnight oats, doner marinade, garlic sauce
- [ ] High-protein yogurts / protein puddings — **6–8 pots** (Brooklea protein range) — snacks
- [ ] Skyr pots — **4** — snacks
- [ ] Light mozzarella — **2 balls / 1 grated bag** — pizza night
- [ ] Reduced-fat grated cheddar — **1 bag** — bagel melts
- [ ] Burger cheese slices — **1 pack** — both burger nights
- [ ] Eggs — **12** — katsu coating, egg fried rice, Sunday bagels
- [ ] Lighter-than-light mayo — **1 jar** — wraps, burger sauce, garlic mayo

## Cereal & cupboard
- [ ] Frosted flakes — **2 boxes** (Harvest Morn) — breakfasts + post-workout bowls
- [ ] Porridge oats — **1 bag** — overnight oats
- [ ] Caramelised biscuit spread — **1 jar** (Bramwells, or actual Lotus Biscoff) — overnight oats
- [ ] Honey — **1 squeezy bottle** (Bramwells) — post-workout, oats
- [ ] Jam — **1 jar** — post-workout bagels
- [ ] Basmati or long-grain rice — **1kg** (or 6–8 microwave pouches if you'd rather not cook it) — katsu, chilli beef, egg fried rice
- [ ] Pasta — **500g** — pesto pasta lunches
- [ ] Reduced-fat green pesto — **1 jar**
- [ ] Panko / golden breadcrumbs — **1 box** — katsu
- [ ] Plain flour — **small bag** — coatings
- [ ] Cornflour — **1 box** — crispy chilli beef, salt & pepper chicken
- [ ] Cornflakes — **small box** — fried chicken crumb (or use the breadcrumbs)
- [ ] Katsu curry sauce — **1 jar/kit** (world foods aisle) — or curry powder + light coconut milk to make your own
- [ ] Soy sauce — **1 bottle**
- [ ] Sweet chilli sauce — **1 bottle** — crispy chilli beef glaze
- [ ] Chilli/garlic sauce — **1 bottle** — kebab night
- [ ] Passata — **1 carton** — pizza sauce
- [ ] Spray oil — **1 bottle** (Solesta) — air fryer everything
- [ ] Ketchup, pickles/gherkins — for burger sauce
- [ ] Spices (check what you have): smoked paprika, garlic granules, cumin, oregano, cayenne, black pepper, **Chinese salt & pepper seasoning** (world foods aisle)

## Frozen
- [ ] Oven fries / skinny fries — **2 bags** — burger nights
- [ ] Potato wedges — **1 bag** — Sunday
- [ ] Peas — **1 bag** — egg fried rice

## Fruit & veg
- [ ] Lettuce (iceberg or gem) — **2** — burgers, wraps, kebabs
- [ ] Tomatoes — **1 pack** + cherry tomatoes **1 punnet** (pesto pasta)
- [ ] Cucumber — **1**
- [ ] Onions — **1 net** + red onions **×2**
- [ ] Peppers — **3-pack ×2** — chilli beef, salt & pepper chicken, pizza
- [ ] Spring onions — **1 bunch**
- [ ] Garlic — **1 bulb** (+ ginger if making the chilli beef glaze fresh)
- [ ] Bananas — **large bunch (7+)** — snacks + post-workout
- [ ] Apples — **1 bag**
- [ ] Grapes — **1 punnet**
- [ ] Sweetcorn — **1 tin** — bagel melts, pizza
- [ ] Jalapeños (jar) — pizza night, optional

## Snack shopping list
- [ ] Protein puddings / high-protein yogurts — counted above, grab extras if they're on offer
- [ ] Protein bars — **1 box** (cereal bar aisle — pick ones with 15g+ protein)
- [ ] Lightly salted popcorn multipack — **1** (Snackrite)
- [ ] Rice cakes — **2 packs** — snacks + post-workout
- [ ] Chocolate — **1–2 bars** (Dairyfine) — a row at a time fits the fat budget fine
- [ ] Jelly sweets — **2 bags** — zero-fat carb top-ups, especially post-workout
- [ ] Dried mango — **2 bags** — post-workout rice cake combo
- [ ] Beef jerky / biltong — **1–2 packs** if in stock — protein snack

## Drinks
- [ ] Orange juice — **2 × 1L** — zero-fat post-workout option
- [ ] Whey protein — Aldi doesn't reliably stock it, so grab your usual from elsewhere; his breakfast shake assumes 1 scoop (~24g protein)

---

**Rough weekly cost:** ~£75–90 for the lot at Aldi prices — the chicken is the biggest line item; swap some breast for thighs to trim it further.
