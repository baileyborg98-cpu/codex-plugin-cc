Here is the test that settles this for most people.

For the next fourteen days, do not change a single thing about how you eat. Just log it. All of it. The Tuesday you were perfect and the Saturday you were not. Then add the fourteen days up and divide by fourteen.

That number is your actual intake. Not your target. Not what you tell people you eat. The number your body has been responding to this whole time.

Almost everyone who runs this gets a shock, and it is always the same shock. The average is nowhere near what they thought they were eating, and the gap is never on the days they were tracking carefully. It is on the days they stopped.

Once you have that number you have something real to work with. Set your target a few hundred below your actual average, not below the fantasy one. It will feel too high. It is supposed to. A target you hit six days out of seven beats a target you hit three days out of seven and then blow apart, every single time.

The people who eat more and lose weight are not gaming their metabolism. They picked a number they could live with, and then they lived with it.
