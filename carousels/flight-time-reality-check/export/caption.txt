Flying Manchester to Alicante tomorrow. 2 hours 40 minutes and I land in a different country, different language, different coastline.

Coming from Australia, that number is hilarious to me.

2h40 from Sydney barely gets you out of your own state. Adelaide if you're lucky. Alice Springs if the wind's right — and you're still dead centre of the country, nowhere near another one. Perth's still five hours away. Darwin's still four.

2h40 got me a new country. That same flight back home won't even get you out of your own state.

Everything is further in Australia. Always has been.
