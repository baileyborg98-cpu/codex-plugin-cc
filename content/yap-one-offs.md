## The book was better

**Format:** Pub yap. Domestic, conspiratorial, you're telling the camera behind her back.

**On screen:** she says the book was better

```
0:00  My wife says the book is better.
0:02  Every film. Every single time.
0:05  And I've got one argument and I reckon it's airtight.
0:08  You were on that book for nine months.
0:11  Nine. Months.
0:13  I put the film on, hour and a half, done,
0:16  and I know everything you know.
0:18  Same story. Same ending. Same bloke dies at the end.
0:22  I just got nine months of my life back.
```

**Land:** She's right. I know she's right. I'm not saying it out loud.

**Delivery:** Low voice, slightly conspiratorial, like you're getting away with something. If she's in the room and within earshot, even better, and if she reacts at any point do not cut it. Her face is the video. "Nine. Months." gets a full stop between the words.

**Why it'll do numbers.** Book versus film is one of the most argued topics on the internet, so the comments fill themselves. The land is what stops it being annoying, because you concede before anyone can tell you you're wrong, and people share a man losing an argument on purpose.
