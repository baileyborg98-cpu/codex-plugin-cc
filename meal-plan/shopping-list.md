# Aldi Shopping List — one week, two people (budget edition)

Covers every meal in [the meal plan](meal-plan.md), plus the snack and post-workout sections. Aldi own-brand names given where they're stable — anything not in stock, the equivalent from the same aisle does the job.

Built to keep the cost down: thigh fillets instead of most of the breast, mince instead of steak strips, wraps doubling as pizza bases, big-tub yogurt instead of individual protein pots.

## Meat & fish
- [ ] Chicken breast fillets — **~1kg** (1 pack) — fajitas + Sunday fried chicken burgers only (the two meals where breast earns its price)
- [ ] Chicken thigh fillets — **~2kg** (2 large packs) — doner wraps, salt & pepper chicken, pizza topping, batch-cooked lunches. Around a third cheaper than breast per kilo
- [ ] 5% lean beef mince — **2 × 500g** — smash burgers + crispy chilli beef (mince version)
- [ ] Bacon medallions — **1 pack** — Sunday bagels
- [ ] Tuna in spring water — **4 tins** — bagel melts, jackets, pasta option

## Bakery (Village Bakery)
- [ ] Brioche burger buns — **1 pack of 4** (Tue + Sun burger nights)
- [ ] Large tortilla wraps — **3 packs of 8** — lunches, fajita night, doner night AND Friday's pizza bases
- [ ] Bagels — **2 packs of 5** — lunches, Sunday breakfast, post-workout
- [ ] White bloomer / thick-sliced loaf — **1** — post-workout toast & honey (does the same job as sourdough for a third of the price)

## Dairy & chilled
- [ ] Semi-skimmed milk — **2 × 4 pints** (Cowbelle)
- [ ] Skimmed milk — **1 × 2 pints** — post-workout cereal bowls
- [ ] Fat-free Greek-style yogurt — **3 × 500g** (Brooklea) — overnight oats, doner marinade, garlic sauce, AND snack bowls with honey
- [ ] Grated mozzarella — **1 bag** — pizza night
- [ ] Cheddar — **1 block** (grate it yourself — cheaper per kilo than pre-grated) — bagel melts, jackets
- [ ] Burger cheese slices — **1 pack** — both burger nights
- [ ] Eggs — **12** — egg fried rice, Sunday bagels, boiled-egg snacks
- [ ] Lighter-than-light mayo — **1 jar** — wraps, burger sauce, garlic mayo

## Cereal & cupboard
- [ ] Frosted flakes — **2 boxes** (Harvest Morn) — breakfasts + post-workout bowls
- [ ] Porridge oats — **1 bag** — overnight oats
- [ ] Caramelised biscuit spread — **1 jar** (Bramwells — the Biscoff dupe, about half the price of Lotus) — overnight oats
- [ ] Honey — **1 squeezy bottle** (Bramwells) — post-workout, oats
- [ ] Jam — **1 jar** — post-workout bagels
- [ ] Basmati or long-grain rice — **1kg dry bag** (a fraction of the price of microwave pouches per portion) — chilli beef, egg fried rice
- [ ] Pasta — **500g** — pesto pasta lunches
- [ ] Reduced-fat green pesto — **1 jar**
- [ ] Cornflakes — **1 box** — crushed for the Sunday fried chicken coating (no need for panko)
- [ ] Plain flour — **small bag** — coatings
- [ ] Cornflour — **1 box** — crispy chilli beef, salt & pepper chicken
- [ ] Soy sauce — **1 bottle**
- [ ] Sweet chilli sauce — **1 bottle** — crispy chilli beef glaze
- [ ] Chilli/garlic sauce — **1 bottle** — kebab night
- [ ] Passata — **1 carton** — pizza sauce
- [ ] Baked beans — **2 tins** (Everyday Essentials) — jacket potatoes
- [ ] Spray oil — **1 bottle** (Solesta) — air fryer everything
- [ ] Ketchup, pickles/gherkins — for burger sauce
- [ ] Spices (check what you have first — these last months): smoked paprika, garlic granules, cumin, oregano, cayenne, black pepper, **Chinese salt & pepper seasoning** (world foods aisle). Paprika + cumin + garlic + cayenne doubles as fajita seasoning, or grab a ~35p sachet

## Snack shopping list
- [ ] Big-tub yogurt + honey covers most protein snacks — already counted above
- [ ] Lightly salted popcorn multipack — **1** (Snackrite)
- [ ] Rice cakes — **2 packs** — snacks + post-workout
- [ ] Chocolate — **1–2 bars** (Dairyfine) — a row at a time fits the fat budget fine
- [ ] Jelly sweets — **2 bags** (own-brand) — zero-fat carb top-ups, especially post-workout
- [ ] Raisins/sultanas — **1 big bag** — post-workout rice cake combo + snacks

## Drinks
- [ ] Orange juice — **2 × 1L** (from concentrate — same carbs, half the price of fresh)
- [ ] Whey protein — Aldi doesn't reliably stock it, so grab your usual from elsewhere; his breakfast shake assumes 1 scoop (~24g protein)

---

**Rough weekly cost:** ~£55–65 if your spice rack and sauce bottles are already stocked (they last months) — roughly £15–20 less than the original list. Prices move about, so treat it as a guide.

### Want it even cheaper?
- **Frozen chicken breast** instead of fresh for the fajitas/burgers — around 25–30% less per kilo, defrost the night before.
- **Whole chicken** (~£4) roasted Sunday instead of some thigh packs — strip the carcass for 3–4 days of lunch meat.
- **Everyday Essentials** versions of pasta, rice, beans, flour and cornflakes — same macros, lowest shelf.
- **Drop the orange juice** — squash with the jelly sweets does the same post-workout job for pennies.
