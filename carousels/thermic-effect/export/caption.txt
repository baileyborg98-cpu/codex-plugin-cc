Before you touch your calories, do this.

For the next fourteen days, change nothing about how you eat. Just log it. All of it. The Tuesday you were perfect and the Saturday you were not. Then add the fourteen days up and divide by fourteen.

That number is your actual intake. Not your target. Not what you tell people you eat. The number your body has been responding to this whole time.

Nearly everyone I put through this gets the same shock. The average is nowhere near what they thought it was, and the gap is never on the days they were tracking carefully. It is on the days they stopped.

Now you have something real to work with. Set your target a few hundred below your actual average, not below the fantasy one. It will feel too high. It is supposed to. A target you hit six days out of seven beats a target you hit three days out of seven and then blow apart, every single time.

You are not going to outsmart your metabolism. You are going to pick a number you can live with, and then live with it. That is the whole thing.
