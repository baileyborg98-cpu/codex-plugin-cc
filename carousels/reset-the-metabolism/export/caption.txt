The hardest part of this is not the food. It is the four weeks where the scale goes up and nothing visible happens.

Week one you gain. Somewhere around one to two percent of your body weight, and none of it is fat. It is glycogen, water and more food physically sitting in you. Week two you feel like you have ruined it. Week three you notice you are sleeping better and your sessions have stopped feeling like wading through mud. Week four you look in the mirror and look the same as you did at the start, and that is exactly the point.

If you want one number to watch while you wait, it is not the scale. It is what you lift. Output coming back up shows in the gym before it shows anywhere else. When your working weights start moving again, you know your body has stopped hiding.

Then you drop. And you drop from 2,000 instead of 1,400.

The reason almost nobody does this is that it asks you to go backwards on purpose for a month with no proof it is working until it already has. That is a lot to ask of someone who has been dieting for a year.

It is still less than another year of cutting calories you do not have.
