# What Worked: the abs reel

Breakdown of the best performing fitness script so far. 57 seconds, 93.6% non followers, comment rate above baseline. This is the structure to reuse.

---

## The script, timestamped

```
00:00  Let me guess, you've been dieting for around eight months
       and you're confused about why you don't have abs
00:05  You don't have abs because you've been dieting for 8 months.
00:08  Hear me out, your body's not stupid.
00:09  You cut the food, you added the cardio, and it did exactly
       what it was built to do.
00:15  It flicked itself into power saving mode.
00:17  And we're going to use your phone as an example.
00:19  When your phone's on 20%, your screen dims, everything shuts
       down just to keep it alive on less energy.
00:26  Now, that's the same with your body.
00:27  Less energy, less movement.
00:29  And now we're burning muscle just to pay for it.
00:31  So you're putting in more than everyone else around you and
       you're getting smaller for it.
00:35  Meanwhile, Jimmy over here, the person you've been comparing
       yourself to is eating 2,700 a day and has a six-pack.
00:43  It has nothing to do with genetics.
00:45  Jimmy is just someone who stopped punishing themselves.
00:47  Jimmy also knows you can't gain fat at maintenance.
00:51  So after this, eat more, train better, and stop being scared
       of a fucking bread rock.
```

---

## The seven moves

**1. Guess their exact situation, with a number.**
"Let me guess, you've been dieting for around eight months." Not "if you're struggling with fat loss." A specific number makes the viewer feel seen and personally accused. The number is the hook, not the topic.

**2. Payoff is the hook sentence reversed.**
Same words, causality flipped. "You don't have abs because you've been dieting for 8 months." No new information required to feel the hit. That reversal is the entire reason the video works.

**3. Buy time out loud.**
"Hear me out, your body's not stupid." Four words that tell someone about to swipe that the explanation is worth it. Cheap and it works.

**4. One metaphor, signposted, extended, bridged back.**
"And we're going to use your phone as an example." Then phone at 20%, screen dims, shuts things down. Then "now, that's the same with your body." Say you're doing a metaphor, do it properly, walk back out. Never two metaphors.

**5. Name the injustice.**
"You're putting in more than everyone else around you and you're getting smaller for it." This is the emotional centre. Not information. Recognition of unfairness. The viewer has felt this and never heard it said.

**6. Give the comparison a name.**
Jimmy. Jimmy appears three times and gets funnier each time. An abstract "other people who have abs" is forgettable. Jimmy is a character, and characters get quoted in comments. This is almost certainly why comment rate came in above baseline.

**7. Close on imperative plus one absurd concrete image.**
"Eat more, train better, and stop being scared of a fucking bread rock." Only swear in the whole script and it lands on the funniest word. Bread rock is doing more work than any motivational line could.

---

## What the curve says

The drop from 100% to 50% happens across the first five seconds and then the line goes nearly flat. From 0:05 to 0:57 it only bleeds from about 50% down to roughly 20%. A tail that flat over 52 seconds means the body of the script was never the problem.

**The only weak spot is 0:00 to 0:05.** Half the audience left before the reversal landed. That reversal is the strongest line in the video and 50% of viewers never heard it.

Fix for next time: get the payoff in by second three. The setup line is currently five seconds long. Trim it to something like "Let me guess, eight months dieting, still no abs" and drop the reversal on top of it immediately. Same script, more people reach the good part.

**Length is not the enemy.** 57 seconds held roughly 20% to the end on a 93.6% cold audience. Stop assuming shorter is safer.

**93.6% non followers** means this travelled well past the existing audience. Content built on a specific accusation plus a reversal is what reaches cold. The deadpan lifestyle stuff serves the people who already know you. Both are needed, they do different jobs.

---

## The template

```
Guess their situation with a specific number
Reverse it, same words, by second three
Buy time in four words
Signpost one metaphor, extend it, bridge back
Name the unfairness they have felt and never heard said
Give the comparison person a name and bring them back twice
Imperative close plus one swear on one absurd concrete image
```

Works for any myth where the effort someone is putting in is the actual cause of the problem. Long dieting, excessive cardio, over restriction, training every day without recovery, tracking obsessively.
