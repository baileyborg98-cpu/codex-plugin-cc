# Beat Batch

Ten scripts built on the four formats. Every one written to a beat clock, because that's what the retention curves said. Nothing sits longer than three seconds without the line, the shot, or the text changing.

Timings are cut points, not a rehearsal script. If a beat runs long when you say it out loud, split it rather than rushing it.

---

# ACCUSATION

Payoff by second three. One metaphor. Jimmy carries across all three so he becomes a character people know.

---

## 1. Cardio

**On screen:** 45 mins of cardio a day

```
0:00  Let me guess, forty five minutes of cardio a day,
0:02  and you're softer now than you were in January.
0:04  You're softer because of the forty five minutes of cardio a day.
0:07  Hear me out, your body's doing maths.
0:09  You gave it less food and more work,
0:11  so it went looking for something to burn,
0:13  and muscle was the cheapest thing in the building.
0:16  You're burning the furniture to heat the house.
0:19  Warm for a bit, then you've got no chairs and you're still cold.
0:23  Meanwhile Jimmy's on three lifts a week and a walk,
0:26  eating more than you, leaner than both of us.
0:29  Jimmy's not gifted.
0:31  Jimmy just stopped setting fire to his own furniture.
```

**Land:** Walk more, and stop torching the chairs.

**Delivery:** New angle every beat through the first ten seconds, that's where you're bleeding. Drop the energy right down on "muscle was the cheapest thing in the building" and let the furniture line sit. Jimmy gets a flat delivery, he's never the punchline, he's just a bloke who worked it out.

## 4. Ten thousand steps

**On screen:** You NEED 10,000 steps a day *(struck through)* actually, not really

```
0:00  If you want to be healthy there's a number,
0:02  and the number is ten thousand.
0:04  Not nine and a half.
0:06  Ten.
0:08  So if you're on nine thousand six hundred at half eleven at night
0:11  you get your shoes back on,
0:13  and you do laps of your kitchen island
0:15  like a man being punished,
0:17  until the watch buzzes.
0:20  Because nine thousand six hundred is a wasted day.
0:24  That's what the watch has got you thinking anyway.
0:27  Ten thousand came from a pedometer advert in the sixties.
```

**Land:** You're doing laps of your kitchen for an advert.

**Comment door:** Tell me the dumbest number your watch has made you chase.

**Delivery:** Film the kitchen laps for real, late, phone on the counter. Completely straight face for the first twenty four seconds, no wink anywhere. The turn is said like you're bored of it.

# OBSERVATION

A claim nobody will argue with, then name the thing in words nobody has used for it, then a flat verdict. Never explain it. This format gets comments for free, so run it most often.

## Batch notes

Jimmy appears in all three accusation scripts. Keep him, he's building into a character people recognise, and a recognisable character is where your comments come from.

Numbers 6 and 7 both carry Delilah and both open on a five something wake up. Don't post them in the same fortnight.

Number 9 uses your age. Update it or drop the number if it dates.

The two satire ones are the only scripts here with a comment door, because they're the only format that needs one. Don't bolt a door onto the observation scripts, the flat verdict is doing that job already.
