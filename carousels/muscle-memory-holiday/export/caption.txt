Can't train on holiday? No worries.

How long can you take off the gym before you start losing muscle?

Your body is constantly both building and breaking down muscle. Lifting weights and eating protein send the signal to build. You only start losing muscle when the balance tips over to breakdown.

So how long does that take? Research has tested it: completely detrained — no workouts, no training at all — it still takes at least 2 to 3 weeks before you start to lose muscle.

And if you're off longer than that and do lose some? It's not as bad as you think, because of muscle memory. When you lift, your muscle cells make permanent changes — little adaptations that stay in the cell even if you shrink. Those changes let you rebuild way faster than starting from zero. What took months to build early on can come back in as little as a few weeks.

So if you miss one or two workouts, relax. Your muscle isn't going anywhere.

And with enough protein and even just one full body workout per week, you can hold onto it for weeks to months.
