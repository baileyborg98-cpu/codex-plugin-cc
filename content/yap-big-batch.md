# Big Batch

Eighteen scripts. Education, lifestyle, motivation. All on a beat clock, nothing sitting longer than three seconds.

**The change to the education ones.** Same engine as the abs reel, opposite direction. Instead of "let me guess, you've been doing this wrong", it's "I did this for years and here's what it cost me." You keep the specific number, the reversal, the one metaphor, the hard close. You lose the finger pointing. It also fits the voice far better, because owning the failure is the thing you actually do.

## 1. Protein

**On screen:** I ate like a bird for two years

```
0:00  I spent two years training six days a week
0:03  and eating about ninety grams of protein.
0:06  Ninety.
0:08  Six foot, lifting heavy, eating like a bird,
0:11  wondering why I looked the same every January.
0:14  Muscle gets built out of something.
0:16  You can't ask a builder for a house
0:18  and hand him a bag of sand.
0:21  Doubled it, changed nothing else,
```

**Land:** The training was never the problem. Go and eat.

**Delivery:** Kitchen, actually making food while you talk. Say "ninety" on its own with a pause either side, that beat is the whole hook.

## Batch notes

The confession open is the fix for the education ones. Every mechanic from the abs reel is still in there, the specific number, the reversal, one metaphor, the hard close, but you're the one who got it wrong, so nobody feels told off. Jimmy doesn't belong in these, he was a device for the accusation format.

Numbers 12 and 18 are close cousins, both about cost. Space them out or pick one.

Number 9 is the best one in here and it needs the least production. Film it the next time it happens.

Numbers 7, 10 and 12 all touch the move. Don't run them in the same fortnight.
