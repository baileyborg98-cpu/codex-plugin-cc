A super simple 6-step framework to help you get lean and stay lean:

1. Go to sleep earlier — cuts the late-night snacking, and better rested means better decisions
2. Don't skip breakfast, push it back — leaves more calories for later in the day
3. Build meals around at least two of: protein, veg, fruit
4. Drink enough water and stay hydrated — cuts the liquid calories
5. Walk anywhere and everywhere — doesn't need to be 10,000 steps, just spread through your day
6. Lift 3 to 5 times a week — build muscle, build strength, give yourself something to show for it

Bonus: stay patient. This shit doesn't happen overnight.
