You're not ready for a fat loss phase yet. Here's why.

Fat loss is an art. It's not as simple as waking up tomorrow, eating less and moving more, and that being all you have to do.

Fat loss is also your habits. It's how well you sleep. It's how well you hydrate. It's the quality of the food you're eating. It's how well you manage your stress.

So what makes you think you can wake up tomorrow, flip the lifestyle you're living right now on its head, and click into a mode where you practise every one of those habits to a much higher standard? Not just for a few weeks. For months. And after the diet finishes too, if you're serious about keeping the result.

If those things aren't part of who you are at the moment, spend at least one month before you enter a calorie deficit improving the fundamentals:

— The quality of the food you eat
— How much you move, day to day and week to week
— Your sleep routine
— Your understanding of the food you're consuming

Once you improve the foundation of your habits, you'll get better results in the fat loss phase, and you'll actually sustain them long term.

We are not in a rush to get in shape. Nobody should be in a rush to get in shape.

Spend one month on that foundation. I have zero doubt you'll thank me once your fat loss phase starts, because it'll come to you so much easier.
