The scariest thing you can do to fix your fat loss is eat more. It is also probably the only thing that will make it work again.

If you are stuck at 1,400 calories, you have to go up before you ever go back down.

Here is why.

A deficit only works while you are eating below the amount your body needs to hold its weight. That gap is the whole thing. But if you started your diet already scraping the floor, there was never much of a gap to begin with.

So you lose a few kilos. You get lighter. And a lighter body needs fewer calories just to run. That is metabolic adaptation. Your body adapts, and your maintenance drops.

Fatigue rises. Stress rises. You move less without noticing. Consistency slips. And now 1,400 is not a deficit any more. It is just your new normal.

That is the forever half diet. Zero fat loss progress. Zero muscle gain progress. Stuck in the middle, paying the full price of dieting and getting none of it back.

So the scale stalls and you think the answer is to drop food again. Except you have nothing left to give. You maxed out your only option on day one.

Where do you go when there is nothing left to take away?

You come back up first, to 2,000 or more. Hold there for a few weeks and let your body settle. If you have spent months or years down there, spend longer eating more.

Then you have room to actually reduce calories again. That is the entire game.

Bringing your calories up is not giving up. It is the only thing that gives you somewhere to go. And you will be far more responsive once you do.
