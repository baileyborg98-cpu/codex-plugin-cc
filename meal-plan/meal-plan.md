# 7-Day Fakeaway Meal Plan (for two)

Quick to cook, tastes like a takeaway, no bland "bodybuilder food". Breakfasts and lunches are kept dead simple so the effort goes into dinner.

All macros are good-faith estimates (±10%) based on typical Aldi UK products — close enough for your ranges without weighing every gram.

## Daily targets

| | Calories | Protein | Fat | Carbs (remainder) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Her** | 2,000–2,300 | 120–140g | ~74g max | ~230–260g |
| **Him** | 2,600–3,000 | 150–180g | ~80g max | ~330–380g |

**Portion rule of thumb:** his plate is roughly 30–40% bigger than hers — mostly extra carbs (rice, bread, fries) and a bit more meat. Fat stays similar for both.

**Fat note:** most days land at 55–75g fat, so you're naturally under the cap. Burger nights (Tue/Sun) are the high-fat days — the plan balances them with low-fat dinners either side. If a day comes in well under on fat and you want it, add: a whole egg, 20g peanut butter, a row of chocolate, or full-fat mayo in place of light.

## Breakfasts (rotate 3)

### B1 — Frosted flakes & milk (+ shake for him)
- **Her:** 50g frosted flakes + 250ml semi-skimmed milk — *~430 kcal with a shake, ~310 without | P 36/12 | C 58 | F 7*
  - Add the whey shake only on days her protein is running low — the plan already hits 120–140g most days without it.
- **Him:** 70g frosted flakes + 300ml semi-skimmed milk + 1 scoop whey in water — *~535 kcal | P 38 | C 78 | F 8*

### B2 — Biscoff overnight oats
Night before: oats + fat-free Greek yogurt + milk + caramelised biscuit spread (Aldi's Bramwells version or actual Biscoff) swirled through, drizzle of honey. Done.
- **Her:** 50g oats, 150g fat-free Greek yogurt, 100ml milk, 15g biscuit spread, 10g honey — *~440 kcal | P 24 | C 57 | F 12*
- **Him:** 70g oats, 200g yogurt, 150ml milk, 20g spread, 15g honey — *~615 kcal | P 34 | C 80 | F 16*

### B3 — Sunday bacon & egg bagels
Bacon medallions (all the taste, a fraction of the fat) + egg + ketchup in a toasted bagel.
- **Her:** 1 bagel, 2 medallions, 1 egg — *~385 kcal | P 25 | C 53 | F 8*
- **Him:** 1½ bagels, 3 medallions, 2 eggs — *~540 kcal | P 37 | C 55 | F 14*

## Lunches (rotate 4 — all under 10 minutes or pre-batched)

### L1 — Chicken salad wraps
Batch-cooked chicken (thigh fillets — cheaper than breast and juicier cold), lighter-than-light mayo, lettuce, tomato, big tortilla(s).
- **Her:** 1 large wrap, 125g chicken — *~420 kcal | P 45 | C 34 | F 8*
- **Him:** 2 wraps, 150g chicken — *~640 kcal | P 55 | C 66 | F 12*

### L2 — Chicken pesto pasta (cook Tuesday night, eat Tue lunch + Wed lunch)
Pasta + batch-cooked chicken + reduced-fat green pesto + cherry tomatoes. Eat warm or cold. (A drained tin of tuna instead of chicken works too and costs less.)
- **Her:** 75g dry pasta, 100g chicken, 25g pesto — *~510 kcal | P 41 | C 56 | F 11*
- **Him:** 110g dry pasta, 130g chicken, 30g pesto — *~700 kcal | P 54 | C 81 | F 13*

### L3 — Tuna & sweetcorn bagel melts
Tuna + light mayo + sweetcorn on bagel halves, grated cheese on top, 5 min under the grill.
- **Her:** 1 bagel, ½ tin tuna, 30g light cheddar — *~430 kcal | P 36 | C 52 | F 8*
- **Him:** 2 bagels, 1 tin tuna, 40g cheddar — *~600 kcal | P 48 | C 74 | F 10*

### L4 — Jacket potatoes (or leftovers)
Microwave 10 min then oven-crisp (or air fryer). Top with tuna-mayo & sweetcorn, or beans & grated cheddar. Pennies per portion.
- **Her:** 1 jacket + tuna-mayo or ½ tin beans + 30g cheddar — *~480–540 kcal | P 20–25 | C 60 | F 8–12*
- **Him:** 1 large jacket + full tin of beans or tuna + 40g cheddar — *~650–700 kcal | P 28–32 | C 85 | F 10–14*
- Protein runs lighter here, so pair it with a yogurt bowl or glass of milk.

Leftovers from last night's fakeaway also slot in here: aim for **her ~450 kcal / him ~620 kcal**.

## Dinners — the fakeaways

All serve 2. Use spray oil + air fryer/oven instead of deep frying — same crunch, half the fat.

### D1 Monday — Loaded chicken fajitas
Chicken breast strips tossed in fajita seasoning (smoked paprika + cumin + garlic granules + cayenne, or a ~35p sachet), seared in a screaming-hot pan with peppers and onions. Warm the wraps, put everything in the middle of the table with grated cheddar, garlic yogurt sauce and salsa/chilli sauce, and build your own.
- **Her:** 2 wraps, 125g chicken, 25g cheddar — *~700 kcal | P 48 | C 74 | F 17*
- **Him:** 3 wraps, 175g chicken, 40g cheddar — *~1,030 kcal | P 70 | C 110 | F 30*

### D2 Tuesday — Smash burgers & fries
5% mince, seasoned, rolled into balls, smashed flat in a screaming-hot pan ~2 min a side. Burger cheese, brioche bun, burger sauce (light mayo + ketchup + chopped pickles), oven fries.
- **Her:** 2 thin patties (200g mince), 1 bun, 1 cheese slice, 150g fries — *~840 kcal | P 58 | C 82 | F 32*
- **Him:** 2 patties (250g mince), 1 bun, 2 cheese slices, 200g fries — *~1,050 kcal | P 66 | C 95 | F 35*

### D3 Wednesday — Crispy chilli beef
Budget version: roll 5% mince into small bite-sized balls, toss in cornflour, air-fry or pan-fry until crispy — half the price of steak strips, same takeaway hit. Glaze: sweet chilli + soy + ketchup + splash of vinegar + garlic & ginger. Toss with fried peppers and onion, serve on rice, spring onion on top.
- **Her:** 200g mince, 180g cooked rice — *~665 kcal | P 44 | C 86 | F 12*
- **Him:** 300g mince, 280g cooked rice — *~960 kcal | P 63 | C 125 | F 17*

### D4 Thursday — Chicken doner kebab wraps
Chicken thighs marinated in yogurt + paprika + cumin + garlic + oregano (even 20 min works). Grill or air-fry, slice thin like doner meat. Load into warm wraps with shredded lettuce, tomato, onion, garlic yogurt sauce and chilli sauce.
- **Her:** 175g thigh, 1 large wrap — *~490 kcal | P 38 | C 41 | F 15* (add 100g fries if hungry: +160 kcal)
- **Him:** 250g thigh, 2 wraps — *~800 kcal | P 54 | C 72 | F 24*

### D5 Friday — Tortilla pizza night
Use the big tortilla wraps you already have as thin-and-crispy bases (no separate purchase needed) + passata mixed with garlic granules & oregano + mozzarella + toppings (cooked chicken, sweetcorn, jalapeños, red onion). Oven 220°C ~6–8 min until the edges crisp. Build-your-own — that's the fun bit.
- **Her:** 1½ wraps, 75g mozzarella, 75g chicken — *~530 kcal | P 45 | C 50 | F 14*
- **Him:** 2 wraps, 100g mozzarella, 100g chicken — *~750 kcal | P 58 | C 70 | F 18*

### D6 Saturday — Salt & pepper chicken + egg fried rice
Chicken thigh chunks (cheaper than breast and crisp up better) in cornflour + salt & pepper seasoning (Chinese aisle), air-fried crispy, tossed with fried onion, pepper, chilli and spring onion. Egg fried rice: day-old rice, egg, peas, soy sauce in a hot pan 4 min.
- **Her:** 175g thigh, 200g rice + egg — *~650 kcal | P 44 | C 72 | F 17*
- **Him:** 250g thigh, 300g rice + egg — *~910 kcal | P 62 | C 105 | F 25*

### D7 Sunday — Fried chicken burgers & wedges 🍗
Butterflied breasts soaked in yogurt/buttermilk (an hour if you can), dredged through seasoned flour + crushed cornflakes (paprika, garlic granules, cayenne, black pepper). Heavy spray of oil, air fryer 200°C ~16–18 min until properly crunchy. Brioche bun, garlic mayo or burger sauce, lettuce, pickles, cheese. Frozen wedges on the side.
- **Her:** 1 breast, 1 bun, 150g wedges — *~855 kcal | P 54 | C 97 | F 19*
- **Him:** 1½ breasts, 1 bun, 250g wedges — *~1,120 kcal | P 70 | C 125 | F 23*

## Post-workout carbs (minimal fat)

Have one of these after training. Mix and match — they're all deliberately low-fat so they don't touch the fat budget.

### Her — 70–80g carbs
1. **Toast & honey:** 3 slices white bloomer toasted + 20g honey — *~73g C, ~370 kcal, 3g F*
2. **Cereal bowl:** 70g frosted flakes + 250ml skimmed milk — *~73g C, ~330 kcal, 1g F*
3. **Bagel & banana:** 1 bagel + 15g jam + 1 banana — *~81g C, ~390 kcal, 2g F*
4. **Rice cakes:** 4 rice cakes + 20g honey + 30g raisins — *~70g C, ~290 kcal, 1g F*
5. **Zero-fat sweet fix:** 300ml orange juice + 50g jelly sweets + an apple — *~80g C, ~330 kcal, 0g F*

### Him — 100g+ carbs
1. **Toast & honey:** 4 slices white bloomer toasted + 30g honey — *~102g C, ~500 kcal, 4g F*
2. **Cereal bowl:** 90g frosted flakes + 300ml skimmed milk + banana — *~115g C, ~530 kcal, 2g F*
3. **Double bagel:** 2 bagels + 20g jam — *~109g C, ~560 kcal, 3g F*
4. **Rice cakes:** 5 rice cakes + 30g honey + 50g raisins — *~100g C, ~410 kcal, 1g F*
5. **Zero-fat sweet fix:** 500ml orange juice + 80g jelly sweets — *~105g C, ~430 kcal, 0g F*

## Snacks (pick 2–3 a day to fill the gaps)

| Snack | Approx macros | Best used for |
|---|---|---|
| Fat-free Greek yogurt bowl (250g) + honey | ~180 kcal, 25g P, 0g F | Protein top-up (big tubs — far cheaper per serving than individual pots) |
| Boiled egg | ~70 kcal, 6g P, 5g F | Protein top-up |
| Glass of milk (300ml) | ~150 kcal, 11g P, 5g F | Protein top-up |
| Banana / apple | ~100 kcal, 25g C, 0g F | Carb filler |
| Lightly salted popcorn (25g bag) | ~110 kcal, 15g C, 4g F | Crunchy snack |
| 2 rice cakes + honey | ~130 kcal, 28g C, 1g F | Carb filler |
| Raisins (30g) | ~90 kcal, 20g C, 0g F | Carb filler |
| Row of chocolate | ~110 kcal, 6g F | Treat — fits the fat budget |
| Jelly sweets (30g) | ~100 kcal, 23g C, 0g F | Zero-fat carbs on big-carb days |
| 20g peanut butter on toast | ~200 kcal, 10g F | For days running low on fat |

Protein puddings and protein bars are handy but pricey per serving — treat them as an occasional grab when they're on offer, not a weekly staple. The yogurt bowl does the same job for about a quarter of the price.

**Daily snack budget:** her ~250–350 kcal, him ~350–500 kcal. Lean toward protein snacks on rest days and carb snacks on training days.

## Sanity check — how a training day stacks up

**Her (Monday):** flakes & milk (310) + chicken wraps (420) + post-workout toast & honey (370) + yogurt & honey bowl (180) + banana (105) + fajitas (700) = **~2,085 kcal, ~140g P, ~50g F** ✔

**Him (Monday):** flakes, milk & shake (535) + chicken wraps (640) + post-workout toast & honey (500) + banana (105) + jelly sweets (100) + fajitas (1,030) = **~2,910 kcal, ~175g P, ~55g F** ✔

On rest days, drop the post-workout carbs and you both land at the lower end of your calorie ranges automatically.
