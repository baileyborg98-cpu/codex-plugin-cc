STOP DIETING HARDER — Instagram carousel exports
=================================================
1080 x 1350 (4:5), ready to post.

stills/  — PNG image of every slide (1-10)
videos/  — 7-second silent MP4 of every slide with its animation playing
caption.txt — post caption

HOW TO POST
1. Instagram > New post > select multiple.
2. For each slide position, pick EITHER the PNG (static) or the MP4 (animated).
   Suggested mix: MP4 for slides 2-8 (the animated diagrams), PNG for 1, 9, 10.
   Using all 10 MP4s also works - videos loop automatically in carousels.
3. Keep them in order 01 -> 10.
4. Paste caption.txt as the caption.
