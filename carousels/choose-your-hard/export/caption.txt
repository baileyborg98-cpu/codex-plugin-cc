Going to the gym is hard. Waking up early is hard. Tracking your food is hard. Saying no to the extra drinks every weekend is hard.

But you know what else is hard?

Going stiff every time someone pulls out a camera. Dreading summer because you do not want to wear a swimsuit. Avoiding mirrors. Starting over every Monday because you quit every Friday.

There is no version of this where life is easy. Both lists cost you something. Neither one lets you off.

You just get to decide which hard you are willing to live with.

One of them leaves you healthier, more confident, more energetic, more magnetic. The other one keeps you exactly where you are, and asks you to do it all again next Monday.

Same effort. Only one of them takes you anywhere.

Choose the hard that gives you the life you actually want.
