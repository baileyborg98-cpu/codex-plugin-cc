Everything you've been told about cheap flights is wrong.

The three lies:

1. "Book on a Tuesday at midnight" — there's no magic day. Demand and flexibility move the price, not the calendar.
2. "Search in incognito" — nobody's hiking the price because you looked twice. Clearing cookies is a comfort blanket, not a strategy.
3. "Book exactly X weeks before" — there's no golden window. Short-haul: weeks out. Long-haul: a few months. A number isn't a plan.

The truth: the price isn't yours to control. It's yours to wait for. Set an alert on the route, stay loose on the dates, and let your number come to you.

That's it. That's the hack.
